Even though it's a Limited Job, Blue Mage does actually have endgame content! We
have access to all of the Savage raids for which we meet the level requirements,
and we can get rewards for completing them.

## Raid Series Overview

We have access to four raid series: **Bahamut**, **Alexander**, **Omega**, and
**Eden**. Each series has unique challenges and rewards.

### Rewards

Completing raid tiers as a full Blue Mage group (synced, Echo off) awards
special achievements and titles.

**Final Floor Achievements**

- **Bahamut**: T5 (Dragon), T9 (Raven), T13 (Dreadwyrm)
- **Alexander**: A4S (Manipulator), A8S (Justice), A12S (Prime)
- **Omega**: O4S (Delta), O8S (Sigma), O12S (Alpha)
- **Eden**: E4S (Gate), E8S (Verse), E12S (Promise)

**Series Meta-Achievements**

- **Bahamut**: Blue Unchained + "Unbound Blue" title
- **Alexander**: Masked Conqueror + "Blue Justice" title
- **Omega**: Omega Blue + "The Azure and Omega" title
- **Eden**: Bluetopia + "Forbidden Blue" title
- **Ultimate**: True Blue achievement + Morbol mount (Bahamut + Alexander)


## Getting Started

### Recommended Raid Order

Difficulty varies by group, but here's a general progression by challenge
level:

1. **T5, T9, A4S** - Easiest; good starting raids
2. **T13, A12S** - Moderate difficulty
3. **A8S** - More challenging
4. **Omega series** - Significantly harder
5. **Eden series** - Most challenging

Your group's comfort with mechanics matters more than the tier number. Some
groups clear A8S easily but struggle with T13.

### Difficulty Considerations

Blue Mage Savage raiding is different from other jobs. Our massive burst damage
lets us skip mechanics and boss phases based on HP thresholds, making Bahamut
and Alexander easier than normal jobs would find them.

However, role-based mechanics become random for us since we're all DPS, making
some fights (especially Omega and Eden) significantly harder. Your success
depends heavily on learning fight mechanics, not gear.

### Prerequisites

**Spells**

You need all spells. This is non-negotiable. Every [role loadout]({{% relref
"/gameplay" %}}) includes {{< spell angel_whisper >}}, which requires beating
30 Masked Carnivale stages. If Masked Carnivale is too hard, you're not ready
for Savage.

Most optimized builds require 100+ spells learned. See the [DPS]({{% relref
"/gameplay/dps" %}}) and [Tank]({{% relref "/gameplay/tank" %}}) guides for
requirements.

**Gear**

Gear matters very little. Just wear level-appropriate gear and execute your
rotation correctly. See [Recommended Gear Sets]({{% relref
"/gearing/gear-sets" %}}) for optimal loadouts, but any synced-level gear works
fine.
