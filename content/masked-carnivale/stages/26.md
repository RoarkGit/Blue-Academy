### Recommended Spells

- Earth filler spell, such as {{< spell plaincracker >}}.

### Required Spells

- {{< spell exuviation >}}
- {{< spell eerie_soundwave >}}

### Act 1

In Act 1 we need to defeat a Mirrorknight. It doesn't really do much, but it
does cast Alternate Plumage which makes it functionally invulnerable to damage.
However, we can remove this with {{< spell eerie_soundwave >}}! Once that's
done, we just need to keep ourselves healed and kill it. It will cast Caber Toss
which can be interrupted with {{< spell flying_sardine >}}.

### Act 2

In Act 2 we fight a somewhat iconic Final Fantasy enemy: Papa Humbaba. He starts
out by casting Raw Instinct which makes all attacks guaranteed critical hits,
but it can be removed with {{< spell eerie_soundwave >}}. He also casts Body
Blow which hits pretty hard, but it's not too bad. Next he will cast Void
Thunder II which is a ground-targeted AOE that spawns Thunderhead clouds. The
clouds will move around the arena and should be avoided as much as possible.
After some time Papa Humbaba will cast Dad Joke which is a large knockback.
Position yourself close to him so that you don't get knocked into the electric
ring around the outside of the arena. He follows this up with Void Thunder III
which applies a nasty Electrocution DOT debuff. Cleanse it from yourself with
{{< spell exuviation >}} and continue killing him. The fight will just loop from
this point, so repeat mechanics as needed until he's dead.
