### Recommended Spells

- {{< spell diamondback >}} or {{< spell exuviation >}}

### Act 1

In Act 1 we have a Viking and Magus to deal with. The Magus is only vulnerable
to physical spells, and the Viking os only vulnerable to magical spells. There
will be some small AOEs to avoid, but other than that they're pretty easy.

### Act 2

In Act 2 we have to deal with a Scribe. It will eventually cast Silence, then
Condensed Libra (which applies a big physical vuln debuff to us), and then
Triple Hit. Silence _needs_ to be interrupted with {{< spell flying_sardine >}}.
After that, we can either remove the vuln debuff from ourselves with
{{< spell exuviation >}} or use {{< spell diamondback >}} to survive. The Scribe
follows up with Mechanogravity and spawns the enemies from Act 1. Avoid the AOEs
and kill all the enemies but make sure to interrupt Silence whenever the Scribe
casts it!

### Act 3

In Act 3 we have to kill an Epilogi. It uses Page Tear, a frontal cleave, and
Magic Hammer, a ground-targeted AOE. Gale Cut spawns small Vacuum Wave clouds
that will eventually blow up in a small AOE. Before they explode, Epilogi will
knock us back with Head Down. Get close to Epilogi and aim yourself towards a
spot with no Vacuum Waves. Eventually Epilogi will cast Bone Shaker and spawn
two Magi which should be killed, since the enemy will do this multiple times and
we don't want to get overwhelmed. The mechanics just repeat from this point on,
so keep going until the Epilogi is dead.
