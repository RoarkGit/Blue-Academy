### Act 1

In Act 1 there are a ton of bombs all over the place. This is our "tutorial"
act. Kill the closest Cherry Bomb and it will set off a chain reaction and kill
the rest of the bombs.

### Act 2

In Act 2 we have 3 sets of bombs and a Progenitrix. If we hit the Cherry Bomb it
will cause a chain reaction that ends in Progenitrix being frozen. Make sure to
be in combat with the Progenitrix _first_ since it will heal up otherwise. The
goal here is just to kill Progenitrix while avoiding the ground-targeted AOEs
and interrupting Burst with {{< spell flying_sardine >}}.

The Progenitrix can be Vibe Checked, but it needs to come from your own Deep
Freeze or Petrify. The freeze from the blue bombs _will not_ work for
{{< spell ultravibration >}}.
