### Act 1

In this stage we need to defeat a cute lil guy, Crom Dubh. He does the same
stuff as other colossi, but he also casts King's Will which makes him bigger,
increases the damage he deals, and decreases the damage he takes. We can
interrupt this with {{< spell flying_sardine >}}.

### Achievement Info

The achievement "The Harder They Fall" is earned by defeating Crom Dubh while
earning the Giant Slayer feat. This is accomplished by allowing him to cast
King's Will three times. Crom Dubh does not cast this below 35% HP, so make sure
to keep him above 35% before then. After casting it three times, he will also
start casting Black Nebula which should be interrupted with
{{< spell flying_sardine >}}. He will also hit _very_ hard at this point, so
keep an eye on your health.
