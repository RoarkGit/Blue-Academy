### Required Spells

- {{< spell loom >}}

### Act 1

In Act 1 there are two Jams behind some walls. We just need to kill these and
then move out of line of sight when they explode.

### Act 2

In Act 2 there are four Jams and a square in the center of the arena. When we
attack _these_ jams they apply a strong Heavy debuff that essentially makes us
immobile. We can get around this by using {{< spell loom >}}! Attack a Jam until
it dies and starts casting The Last Song, and then use {{< spell loom >}} to
move out of its line of sight using the center square. Repeat this for each Jam.
