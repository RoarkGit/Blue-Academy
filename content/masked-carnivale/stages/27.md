### Act 1

Typhon is back and a lot more difficult this time. This stage gave me the most
difficulty when I first did the Masked Carnivale. Typhon spawns a bunch of Bombs
around the arena as well as a Magitek Explosive. If the Magitek Explosive is hit
by a Bomb or a Fireball from Typhon, it will detonate and instantly kill us. The
Magitek Explosive will also detonate on its own after awhile, so we need to kill
it before that happens. Its weakness is Water so it is important to use Water
damage to kill it as quickly as possible.

The basic idea here is that we want to pull Bombs _away_ from the Magitek
Explosive and kill the Magitek Explosive. We want to bring bombs _towards_
Typhon since the Bomb explosion will deal damage to Typhon as well, and Typhon
has a _ton_ of health. Each Bomb will deal about 10% of Typhon's health, so it
doesn't take too many bombs to kill him. There are a few different
configurations of Bomb and Magitek Explosive spawns so it can take some practice
to get used to them. You can reference the video above to see what the different
setups are. Whenever a set of Bombs spawns, quickly look to see if there are any
near the Magitek Explosive and pull them away with {{< spell sticky_tongue >}}.
Once they're out of the way, kill the Magitek Explosive but don't be too close
to it or Typhon's Fireballs can detonate it! Typhon will use spells like Snort
and Fungah to move the Bombs and you around, so it will take a few tries to
really know how to handle each configuration.

## Alternative Strat (requires Hydro Pull)

{{< youtube M9pJzf_UGa8 >}}

{{< spell hydro_pull >}} makes the stage _significantly_ easier as shown in the
video above. {{< spell hydro_pull >}} allows you to pull multiple Bombs onto
Typhon at once, and then use {{< spell hydro_pull >}} to kill the Magitek
Explosive. If you are going to do it this way, make sure that you're always
closer to the Magitek Explosive than you are to Typhon so that the Magitek
Explosive takes the full damage and not 50% reduced damage as a secondary
target!
