### Act 1

In Act 1 there are four Grenades sleeping around the perimeter of the room.
These detonate when attacked, so just attack them all (while staying out of
range) to blow them up!

### Act 2

In Act 2 we have to deal with The Forefather. This is mechanically similar to
the Progenitrix in {{< stage 8 >}} but with some extra mechanics. He will cast
Sap which we need to dodge, and eventually a bunch of ground-targeted AOEs will
cover the ground. We need to quickly find the safe spot and move to it. The
Forefather will then cast Bombshell Drop and spawn a Grenade which we need to
kill before The Forefather blows it up with Ignition. The mechanics will loop a
bit until he spawns a Gas Bomb and starts channeling Burst. Burst cannot be
interrupted with {{< spell flying_sardine >}}, but we _can_ interrupt the cast
by pulling the Gas Bomb on top of The Forefather with
{{< spell sticky_tongue >}} and letting it blow up. When it casts Flashthoom it
stuns The Forefather and interrupts the Burst cast. At this point the fight just
loops until The Forefather is dead.
