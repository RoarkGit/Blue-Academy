### Act 1

In Act 1 we have two Imps to kill. They cast Void Blizzard which can be
interrupted and Icefall which can be dodged.

### Act 2

In Act 2 we have to defeat a Chimera, which is the same as basically every other
Chimera in the game. If it casts The Ram's Voice, move away. If it casts The
Dragon's Voice, get close. The Chimera will also cast The Ram's Keeper which
puts a frost puddle on the ground, so just avoid it or interrupt it with
{{< spell flying_sardine >}}. Eventually two Imps will spawn and these can be
handled the same as in Act 1.

The Imps in this stage can be Vibe Checked.
