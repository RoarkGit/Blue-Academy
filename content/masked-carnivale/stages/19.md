### Recommended Spells

- {{< spell ink_jet >}} makes this stage _significantly_ easier, but it is not
  strictly required.

### Act 1

In Act 1 Reflective Rebekkah will cast Reflect, which will cause any magic
damage she takes to be reflect back at us, so we need to kill her with physical
spells. Dodge the AOEs and interrupt Offal Breath with
{{< spell flying_sardine >}} (or dodge it) while killing her. She hits pretty
hard overall, so watch your health.

### Act 2

In Act 2 we are fighting Reflective Rebekkah again but there is one additional
mechanic. Eventually she will cast Schizocarps which spawns a bunch of Hot Hips
around the arena that start casting a gaze attack. If you remember how Blind
worked in {{< stage 6 >}}, we can do the same thing here! If we cast
{{< spell ink_jet >}} on Reflective Rebekkah we will apply Blind to ourselves
and then we can completely ignore the gaze mechanic. If you don't have
{{< spell ink_jet >}} you can go to the very edge of the arena and look directly
between two Hot Hips to avoid the gaze attack. This is pretty tight, though, so
I recommend just using {{< spell ink_jet >}}.
