### Recommended Spells

- {{< spell diamondback >}}

### Act 1

Act 1 has two slow-moving Cyclops enemies that will one-shot us if they get
close. All we need to do is stay out of melee range and kill them. They are
vulnerable to Heavy effects, so spells like {{< spell reflux >}} can be helpful
to slow them down even further.

### Act 2

In Act 2 we need to deal with Tikbalang which is the same as any other minotaur
in the game. It will cast 10-tonze Slash which is a cone AOE and 111-tonze Swing
which is a point-blank AOE. After a bit of time it will cast Voice of Authority
and spawn one of the Cyclops enemies from Act 1, which needs to be killed.
Tikbalang will eventually cast a gaze attack called Cry of Rage followed by The
Bull's Voice which gives it a critical hit rate up buff. It will then draw us in
and apply a Heavy, then start casting 1111-tonze Swing, a _massive_ point-blank
AOE. If Tikbalang is at the edge of the arena we can use something like
{{< spell loom >}} or {{< action sprint >}} to get to the safe side, but we can
also just stand still and use {{< spell diamondback >}}. Tikbalang will then
charge at us with a knockback, so try to position yourself such that you don't
get knocked into the electric edge of the arena. Tikbalang will then cast
10-tonze Wave which is a donut AOE, and then repeat mechanics after that. There
is quite a bit going on in this stage and it can take a few tries to complete
it.
