### Recommended Spells

- Water filler spell, such as {{< spell water_cannon >}}

### Required Spells

- {{< spell ice_spikes >}} or {{< spell schiltron >}}
- A Stun spell, such as {{< spell faze >}}

### Act 1

In Act 1 there are four Roselets around the arena. Cast {{< spell ice_spikes >}}
and aggro one of the Roselets. All of the Roselets will attack you, but the
reflected damage from {{< spell ice_spikes >}} will immediately kill them.

### Act 2

In Act 2 we need to kill the Hydnora which is somewhat similar to a Behemoth. It
periodically uses narrow cone AOEs that can be sidestepped. It will eventually
cast Spore Sac which spawns a bunch of Roselets, so just put up
{{< spell ice_spikes >}} again. When Hydnora casts Infallible Fumes you can
interrupt the cast by stunning it with {{< spell faze >}}.
