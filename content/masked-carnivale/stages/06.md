### Recommended Spells

- An AOE filler spell and a Lightning filler spell. {{< spell electrogenesis >}}
  covers both of these!

### Act 1

In Act 1 we need to defeat two Mandragoras and two Catoblepas. The Catoblepas
will use gaze/look away attacks, but we can actually ignore this entirely by
intentionally getting hit by the Mandragoras' Teary Twirl! This attack applies a
Blind effect which incidentally makes us immune to gaze attacks. Once we are
blinded, we can just kill the enemies.

### Act 2

Act 2 is similar to Act 1 but we also have two Ahriman to deal with. The Ahriman
will cast Stone which is unavoidable, so just make sure to stay healed up.

The Catoblepas cannot be Vibe Checked, but the other enemies can.
