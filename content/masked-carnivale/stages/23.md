### Act 1

If you've ever seen a Behemoth you know what to expect here. The Behemoth casts
Charybdis which spawns a tornado that we need to stay away from. It will also
cast Trounce, a narrow cone AOE, that is easily sidestepped. Eventually the
Behemoth will cast Comet and spawn a bunch of circular AOEs that need to be
dodged. After a long time it will start casting Ecliptic Meteor.. but the
problem is we don't have any meteors to hide behind like with other Behemoths!
Luckily we have {{< spell diamondback >}} which allows us to survive. The whole
fight loops from this point and doesn't get any more difficult.
