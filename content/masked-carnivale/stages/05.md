### Act 1

In this stage all we need to do is kill three turtles. However, they have a
massive damage reduction buff that makes us do essentially no damage! Luckily we
have some tools for that. {{< spell 1000_needles >}} _always_ does 1,000 damage
regardless of mitigation, so we can kill them with that. Death effects like
{{< spell doom >}} can also work!

All enemies in this stage can be Vibe Checked, just be aware of the range.
