### Act 1

In Act 1 we have two Claw enemies to defeat. The Left Claw has a Repelling Spray
buff that reflects magic damage, and the Right Claw has a Repelling Spray debuff
that reflects physical damage (yes, they're both called Repelling Spray). Use
the opposing damage type to defeat each Claw and be careful about AOE to avoid
reflecting damage back onto yourself. The Claws will cast some short-range line
and cone AOEs that are easily avoided, so they're not too scary overall.

### Act 2

Kreios from {{< stage 2 >}} returns for this stage and does the same stuff as
before. Avoid his AOEs while killing him. Eventually he will spawn the Claws
from Act 1. Kill the Claws while avoiding the AOEs and then go back to Kreios
himself. Kreios casts Magitek Ray, which is a ground-targeted AOE that leaves
behind a fire puddle. Magitek Ray can be interrupted with
{{< spell flying_sardine >}} or it can just be dodged entirely.

All enemies in this stage can be Vibe Checked, _including Kreios_ in Act 2.
