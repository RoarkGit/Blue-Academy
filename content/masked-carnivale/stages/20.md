### Recommended Spells

- Fire filler spell, such as {{< spell bomb_toss >}}
- {{< spell final_sting >}}
- {{< spell whistle >}}
- {{< spell moon_flute >}}
- {{< spell off-guard >}}

### Required Spells

- {{< spell diamondback >}}

### Act 1

In Act 1 we need to defeat a Typhon. This isn't too bad and mostly involved
dodging Fireballs. Eventually Typhon will cast Snort followed by three
unavoidable Fireball casts. It _is_ possible to heal through these very quickly,
but it is _much_ easier to just use {{< spell diamondback >}} when the Snort
cast starts.

### Act 2

In Act 2 we need to defeat Ultros. We just need to dodge some line AOEs and
interrupt Imp Song with {{< spell flying_sardine >}}.

### Act 3

In Act 3 we need to fight Typhon _and_ Ultros at the same time. They do the same
mechanics as before, but now Ultros will also spawn four tentacles that make
things even more chaotic. If you have the recommended spells above, you can
cheese Act 3 and get an achievement.

### Achievement Info

The achievement "Octopath Traveler" is earned by completing this stage while
earning the Trouble with Tentacles feat. This is accomplished by completing the
stage without killing any Ultros tentacles. This sounds difficult but it's
actually really easy. If we use a fully buffed {{< spell final_sting >}} combo
on Typhon in Act 3, he will die immediately and we get the achievement. This is
significantly easier than doing the stage the normal way and dealing with the
tentacles, so I recommend just doing this.

If you are failing to kill Typhon with the {{< spell final_sting >}} combo I can
almost guarantee you have {{< spell mighty_guard >}} on or something. Every
patch someone says it doesn't work anymore, but it still does. If it's not
working _for you_, that means _you_ are doing something wrong.
