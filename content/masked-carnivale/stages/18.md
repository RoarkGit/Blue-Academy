### Act 1

In Act 1 we have one Manticore and a bunch of barrels. The Manticore does the
same things as other manticores in the game: a swipe attack, a charge attack
with a knockback, and a fireball attack. If barrels take damage they will
explode and deal a ton of damage to all nearby targets, including the Manticore.
The Manticore's damage can _also_ blow up the barrels. The goal is to lead the
Manticore around the arena and detonate barrels next to it until it dies. If
there are still barrels up when the Manticore dies, we just need to blow them up
to move onto the next stage.

### Act 2

In Act 2 we have two Manticores! The process here is the same but we need to
dodge attacks from both of them. The Manticores will also damage each other, so
we want to try to keep them close together if possible.

All enemies in this stage can be Vibe Checked.
