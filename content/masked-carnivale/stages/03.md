### Recommended Spells

- Water filler spell, such as {{< spell water_cannon >}}.

### Act 1

In this stage we just need to kill Zipacna. It casts a ground-targeted AOE
called Earthen Heart that can be interrupted with {{< spell flying_sardine >}},
or we can just avoid the damage entirely by moving out of the way. It eventually
casts Obliterate which _should_ be interrupted, but it won't one-shot you,
either.
