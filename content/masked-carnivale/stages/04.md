### Recommended Spells

- Lightning filler spell, such as {{< spell electrogenesis >}}.
- Fire filler spell, such as {{< spell bomb_toss >}}

### Act 1

In Act 1 we just need to kill the bats and the wolf. They do hit pretty hard, so
make sure to keep yourself healed up while they hit you.

### Act 2

In Act 2 we need to kill Kreios who does the same stuff as any other colossus in
the game. There will be Grand Strike line AOEs that we need to dodge and also a
Magitek Field cast to interrupt. Eventually he will spawn a bunch of Beetles but
they can be ignored.

All enemies in this stage can be Vibe Checked.
