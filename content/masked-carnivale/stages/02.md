### Recommended Spells

- Water filler spell, such as {{< spell water_cannon >}}.
- Wind filler spell, such as {{< spell sonic_boom >}}.
- Earth filler spell, such as {{< spell plaincracker >}}.
- Lightning filler spell, such as {{< spell electrogenesis >}}.
- Fire filler spell, such as {{< spell bomb_toss >}}.
- Ice filler spell, such as {{< spell northerlies >}}.

### Act 1 & 2

This stage is the tutorial stage for elemental aspects. You will want to use a
spell that corresponds to each slime's weakness, which is visible over their
heads. All we need to do is kill each slime! Eventually they will cast Golden
Tongue which increases their damage. It's not particularly threatening, but you
can interrupt it with {{< spell flying_sardine >}}.

All enemies in this stage can be Vibe Checked.
