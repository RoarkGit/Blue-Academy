### Recommended Spells

- {{< spell hydro_pull >}}

### Act 1

Act 1 has two Gas Bombs chain casting Fulmination. Whenever they take damage
they get knocked back and the cast restarts. If the cast finishes, we die. We
simply need to keep attacking them to interrupt the casts until they're dead!

### Act 2

Act 2 is the same as Act 1 except we have four Gas Bombs. We can just attack and
switch targets like in Act 1, but we can make our lives even easier by just
standing in the middle and spamming {{< spell hydro_pull >}}. This will hit all
the Gas Bombs and pull them towards you, so you can just spam it until they're
dead.
