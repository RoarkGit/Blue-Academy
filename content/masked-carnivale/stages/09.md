### Recommended Spells

- Water filler spell, such as {{< spell water_cannon >}}.
- Wind filler spell, such as {{< spell sonic_boom >}}.
- Earth filler spell, such as {{< spell plaincracker >}}.
- Lightning filler spell, such as {{< spell electrogenesis >}}.
- Fire filler spell, such as {{< spell bomb_toss >}}.
- Ice filler spell, such as {{< spell northerlies >}}.

### Act 1

This stage is a bit similar to {{< stage 2 >}} but there is one "boss" slime,
the Guimauve. This casts a ground-targeted AOE called Dark which leaves behind a
puddle -- just avoid it. It will cast Golden Tongue, which increases its damage,
so interrupt it with {{< spell flying_sardine >}}. Puddings will spawn
periodically with different elemental weaknesses. They die in just a few spell
casts, so kill them with their weakness and then go back to killing Guimauve.
