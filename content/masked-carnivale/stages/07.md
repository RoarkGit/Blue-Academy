### Required Spells

- {{< spell sticky_tongue >}}

### Act 1

In Act 1 we have one Lava Slime and two Ice Sprites in front of us. This is
essentially the "tutorial" act. Kill the Lava Slime and it will explode, killing
the two Ice Sprites. Just don't be near the Lava Slime when it explodes!

### Act 2

In Act 2 we have four Lava Slimes and four Ice Sprites. We just need to use
{{< spell sticky_tongue >}} to pull each slime near an Ice Sprite, then move
away from the Lava Slime and kill it.

### Act 3

In Act 3 we have six Lava Slimes and two Gladatorial Nodes. The nodes will cast
a lethal AOE periodically, but it can be avoided by moving out of line of sight.
We can use the walls to get out of line of sight. We need to pull the slimes
next to the nodes and blow them up. It takes three total slimes to kill each
node. Slimes can be pulled between the nodes to hit both at once which can save
a bit of time or cover any mistakes in case you don't pull slimes close enough
to the nodes.
