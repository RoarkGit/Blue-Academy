### Act 1

In Act 1 we just have two petrified Vodorigas and one Haagenti to kill. Kill
them while avoiding the AOEs!

### Act 2

In Act 2 we need to kill Carmilla, who functions the same as any other succubus
enemy you've seen before. Dodge her AOEs, look away from the gaze attacks, and
interrupt Beguiling Mist with {{< spell flying_sardine >}}. Eventually she will
spawn a Succubus add which we need to kill quickly. Just like in Haukke Manor
(Hard), if we don't kill the add she will consume it and kill us!

The enemies in Act 1 can be Vibe Checked.
