## Requirements
The requirements to unlock Blue Mage are pretty straightforward:
- Have at least one combat class/job at level 50.
- Complete the main scenario quest [The Ultimate Weapon](https://na.finalfantasyxiv.com/lodestone/playguide/db/quest/2ba6b3e60da/)
