## Requirements

The requirements to unlock Blue Mage are pretty straightforward:

- Have at least one combat class/job at level 50.
- Complete the main scenario quest
  [The Ultimate Weapon](https://na.finalfantasyxiv.com/lodestone/playguide/db/quest/2ba6b3e60da/).

## NPC

Once you have met the above requirements, you can start the quest
[Out of the Blue](https://na.finalfantasyxiv.com/lodestone/playguide/db/quest/667c7f49a11/)
from the
[Zealous Yellowjacket](https://na.finalfantasyxiv.com/lodestone/playguide/db/npc/npc/03268c7aeb5/)
in Limsa Lominsa (x:9.9, y:11.0).

The quest itself is pretty straightforward, just like any other quest to unlock
a new job. The general vibe of Blue Mage quests is pretty goofy and reminds me a
bit of Hildibrand. If you enjoyed those quests, you'll probably enjoy the Blue
Mage quests so I would recommend following along with the dialogue and
cutscenes!
