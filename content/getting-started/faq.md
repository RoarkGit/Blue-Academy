## Spell requirements for group content

Tank and healer builds have the **least spell requirements**.
DPS is the most demanding role.

**Minimum requirements by role:**

- **Tanking:** {{< spell mighty_guard >}} only
- **Healing:** {{< spell pom_cure >}} or {{< spell stotram >}} only
- **DPS:** Varies widely - benefits most from additional spells

**Dungeons:**

- {{< spell the_rams_voice >}} and {{< spell ultravibration >}}
  make it much easier
- Beyond that, anything goes

**Higher-tier content:**

- DPS should learn as many Primal spells as possible
  ({{< spell feather_rain >}}, {{< spell surpanakha >}},
  {{< spell nightbloom >}}, etc.)

**Spell-learning content:**

- Tank and healer builds are viable and have the least spell requirements
- {{< spell angel_whisper >}} is not required for healers
- Weak DPS is the bottleneck

## Spell learning mechanics

For a detailed guide, see the [Learning Spells]({{% ref
"learning-spells" %}}) page.

**Basic requirements:**

- The enemy casts the spell before it dies
- The enemy actually dies
- You are alive when the enemy dies

**Things that affect learning rate:**

- Spell star rating (fewer stars = higher success rate)
- **Level syncing guarantees a 100% learn rate for dungeons/trials/raids**

**Things that do NOT affect learning rate:**

- Number of times the enemy casts the spell
- Number of Blue Mages in the party
- Having non-Blue Mages in the party
- Party item level (minimum ilvl does nothing)
- Getting hit by the spell
- Silencing Echo

**Unsynced learn rates:** 1-star spells ~50%, 5-star spells ~5%.
Running synced is almost always better if possible.

**Special cases:**

- Quest spells ({{< spell mind_blast >}}, {{< spell glower >}})
  have 100% learn rate regardless of level sync
- Masked Carnivale uses unsynced learn rates

## Mortal Flame: A DOT with special mechanics

{{< spell mortal_flame >}} is a **single-target DoT** applied
once per target. Only one player maintains it - others will
overwrite it.

**Key mechanics:**

- Can only be applied in combat (won't initiate combat)
- Snapshots all active buffs at application time
- Persists until target dies or leaves combat

**When to use {{< spell mortal_flame >}}:**

{{< spell mortal_flame >}} becomes more potent than
{{< spell sonic_boom >}} after:

| Buff Status | Time to Break Even |
|-------------|-------------------|
| No buffs | 18 seconds active |
| Full buffs* | 9 seconds active |

*Full buffs = {{< spell moon_flute >}}, {{< spell bristle >}},
{{< spell off-guard >}}, {{< spell peculiar_light >}}

**Who should bring it?**

- Second-highest DPS in your group
- Any DPS-role player (Mimicry)
- **NOT tanks** - never use on tank builds

**In the opener:**

Apply {{< spell mortal_flame >}} after all group buffs are
active for maximum snapshot value.

## Stat squish

**Why is my HP/damage lower than older videos?**

In Endwalker, all combat values were reduced ~60% for both
players and enemies. Both sides scaled equally, so relative
difficulty stayed the same.

**Does this mean I'm weaker?**

No. Everything was changed proportionally.

**Does this mean Carnivale is harder?**

No. Everything was changed proportionally.

**But it really feels like I'm getting hit harder or doing less damage!**

That's a feeling, not reality. The balance is identical to before.

## Apokalypsis vs Being Mortal

{{< spell apokalypsis >}} deals more potency than
{{< spell being_mortal >}} but requires you to stand still for its
full channel duration, making it risky in most content. In practice,
{{< spell being_mortal >}} is the safer default.

The exception is the [Even/Odd opener](/gameplay/moon-flute-opener/#evenodd-opener),
where you Moon Flute every minute and can plan your movement around the
openers. In that setup you alternate {{< spell phantom_flurry >}} and
{{< spell apokalypsis >}} as your finisher, using one per window, which
lets you get the full potency of both without channeling them back to
back.

## Blue Mage Log

**How do I unlock the Blue Mage Log?**

Do your job quests.

**What are the rewards for completing entries in the Blue Mage Log?** 

- Fame (achievements and titles)
- Fortune (Tomestones of Poetics and Allied Seals)

**What are the requirements for Blue Mage Log completion?** 

To get credit for the Blue Mage Log, your group must be:
* Level synced
* Full
* Only Blue Mages

The "avoid Unrestricted Party" message is misleading. You
don't just avoid it - you MUST avoid it to get log credit.

**What happens if someone leaves?** 

Requirements are checked at entry. If you meet them when
entering, you get log credit even if someone leaves during.

## Death spell accuracy

Based on testing by Rhesh'a Tayuun and Mana Invenbel:

| Spell | Accuracy |
|-------|----------|
| {{< spell launcher >}} | 100% |
| {{< spell dimensional_shift >}} | 100% |
| {{< spell level_5_death >}} | 90% |
| {{< spell missile >}} | 67% |
| {{< spell doom >}} | 33% |
| {{< spell tail_screw >}} | 15% |

All death spells share resistances - if one fails, all will fail on that enemy.

**What about {{< spell ultravibration >}}?**

Not technically a death spell. If an enemy can be
frozen/petrified, {{< spell ultravibration >}} works 100%.

**Which death spell should I use?**

- **Dungeons:** {{< spell ultravibration >}} for trash,
  {{< spell level_5_death >}} for bosses
- **Raid adds:** {{< spell level_5_death >}} (quick kills),
  {{< spell missile >}} (spam), {{< spell dimensional_shift >}}
  (fallback)
- **Avoid:** {{< spell doom >}} and {{< spell tail_screw >}} - too unreliable
