## Blue Mage Log

**How do I unlock the Blue Mage Log?** 
Do your job quests.

 **What are the rewards for completing entries in the Blue Mage Log?** 
- Fame (achievements and titles)
- Fortune (Tomestones of Poetics and Allied Seals)

 **What are the requirements for Blue Mage Log completion?** 
To get credit for the Blue Mage Log, your group must be:

* Level synced
* Full
* Only Blue Mages

The "it is recommended that you avoid using the Unrestricted Party" message is misleading. It's not just recommended that you avoid it, you need to avoid it to get log completion.

 **What happens if someone leaves?** 
The requirements are only checked when you enter the duty. As long as you fulfilled the requirements when you started the duty, you will still get log credit even if someone leaves during the duty.

## Death spell accuracy

** Death Spell Accuracy **
While we have no official confirmation, Rhesh'a Tayuun and later Mana Invenbel did a bunch of testing for us to approximate some values:

**Launcher:** 100%
**Dimensional Shift:** 100%
**Level 5 Death:** 90%
**Missile:** 67%
**Doom:** 33%
**Tail Screw:** 15%

Missile has a different hit rate depending on the caster and target level, but this is not universal among the death spells.  (For example, we could not determine a difference in hit rate at any level after thousands of casts for Tail Screw.)  For more information or to contribute more data, see this thread: https://discord.com/channels/762797677133561887/1326373550810206218

All of these spells share resistances --- for example, you will never see Launcher fail on an enemy if Doom works.

** What about Ultravibration? **
This is *technically* not a death spell. If an enemy can be frozen/petrified, Ultravibration will work on it 100% of the time.

** Which death spell is best? **
This depends on the situation. If you're running dungeons, Ultravibration is best for trash mobs because of its short cooldown. Level 5 Death is great if you need to kill enemies very quickly but infrequently (e.g. T5 adds, T13 Meracydia adds). Missile is generally best if you need to be killing lots of things and spamming the spell (e.g. T9 golems, T13 intermission adds). Dimensional Shift can be okay for AOE packs, but its slow cast time makes it usually not worth it due to Level 5 Death or Ultravibration simply doing a better job. If they're on cooldown then Dimensional Shift can be useful. Doom and Tail Screw are almost never worth it due to their accuracy.

## Gavel Skip (with apok)

**Skipping Gavel at 80/"Apokalypsis Skip"**  
Since 5.45 (level 70 patch), gavel skip has been possible in a few ways, all involving Final Sting.  This causes timing issues, as a player dying during the gavel sequence causes an instant raid wipe.  Thus, the timing for using Final Sting is very tight (less than a GCD).  This is why this server generally recommends to just do the gavel mechanic instead of attempting a skip.

In 6.45 (level 80 patch), it became possible to skip gavel *without* using sting, and using Apokalypsis to minimize downtime during waning.  I (Gwen is writing this) am only aware of one such clear, and there are no logs from the clear as far as I know.  The person who posted the no-sting kill did post some notes [here](<https://discord.com/channels/762797677133561887/762800731093991475/1193799506836340837>) and PoV videos [here](<https://discord.com/channels/762797677133561887/762800896810942484/1193790185582907512>).

If anyone knows of another no-sting gavel skip using apok, especially one with logs, I'd love to know about it.  Discussion on using apok as a gavel skip tool is also a little clouded, since there are a few videos of "Apok gavel skip" that _still_ require people to sting (and thus still have all the timing issues associated with stinging), so I'm trying to emphasize the "no-sting" aspect of this clear.

 **Should I do this?**  
Gavel isn't that hard compared to modern raid mechanics, and the person who posted the clear vid said that it took them longer to prog this than to just learn gavel.  We also don't have a lot of examples of this being done to establish just how difficult it is to pull off.  If you're aiming for a "safe" route, it's _probably_ easiest to just learn gavel even still, but if you like experimental methods... have fun and report back. 😉

## Getting started with Blue Mage

**What is the best order to do things when getting started?** 
The general recommendation is to do the following:
1. Reach level cap; this is easiest with help from a level 100 player but can be done quickly even solo; refer to [server channel] for more info.
2. Learn all overworld (non-duty) spells.
3. Pick up totem spells that are currently available to you. The important ones are White Wind and Mighty Guard.
4. Learn Aetheric Mimicry from the first boss in Pharos Sirius (Hard), this is easily done solo and unsynced since adds will keep spawning until you learn it.
5. Start learning spells from 4-player duties either solo or in a party. There is a reference image for soloing in [server channel]. You should also start Masked Carnivale and do whatever stages you are capable of doing.
6. Start learning spells from 8-player duties in a party. This is easiest if you do it in expansion order (starting with ARR). You should also try to finish Masked Carnivale and learn Angel Whisper.

Throughout each step you should be trying to complete your job quests. Spells that are required for job quests are a guaranteed learn from dungeons even if unsynced. Gear doesn't matter much and you can just use tomestone gear once you hit level cap.

## Mortal Flame

**How does Mortal Flame work?** 
Mortal Flame is a damage-over-time (DoT) effect that can only be applied _once per target_, and will persist until the target dies or leaves combat.  This means that _only one player_ in combat with a target (1/4 for dungeons, 1/8 for trials/raids, 1/24 for alliance raids, 1/∞ for open-world mobs) can apply it, and anyone else using Mortal Flame on the same target will overwrite the previous application.  This overwriting will occur regardless of the existing application being stronger due to more snapshotted buffs.

Mortal Flame can only be used _while already in combat_; if you attempt to use Mortal Flame to initiate combat, it will do nothing.  Mortal Flame is used in the opener, once all buffs are active.

 **How do damage buffs work with Mortal Flame?** 
Mortal Flame will snapshot all buffs active at the time of application.  These buffs will persist for the entire duration Mortal Flame is active (typically, until the target dies).

 **When is Mortal Flame worth using?** 
This will depend on how long combat is expected to last, and which spell Mortal Flame is being used in place of.

A non-buffed Mortal Flame will surpass a 220p filler gcd after 18s active.

A maximally-buffed Mortal Flame (Moon Flute, Bristle, Off-guard, Peculiar Light, Astral Attenuation) will surpass a 220p filler gcd after 9s active.

 **Who should use Mortal Flame?** 
Typically, it is brought by the second-highest DPS player in your group.  Any _DPS-mimicry player_ is fine, as long as they apply Mortal Flame in the opener under full buffs available from the group and themselves.

 **Should BLU tanks use Mortal Flame?** 
No, never under any circumstances.

## Spell learning mechanics

**Requirements for learning spells** 
> The enemy casts the spell before it dies.
> The enemy actually dies.
> You are alive when the enemy dies.

 **Things that affect learning rate** 
> Spell star rating (generally speaking, fewer stars = higher success rate)
> **Level syncing (for dungeons/trials/raids, you are guaranteed to learn the spell when running synced)**

 **Things that do not affect learning rate** 
> Number of times the enemy casts the spell.
> Number of Blue Mages in the party.
> Having non-Blue Mages in the party.
> Party item level (minimum ilvl does *nothing*)
> Getting hit by the spell.
> Silencing Echo.

 **What is the unsynced learn rate for spells?** 
We do not know exact numbers, but we have enough anecdata that it's safe to assume most 1-star spells are
around a 50% learn rate, and most 5-star spells are about a 5% learn rate. For every person whose best
friend's roommate's girlfriend (who plays on a different server, you wouldn't know her) learned every spell
unsynced on the first try there will also be someone who ran Shiva 50 times and didn't learn it.

The learn rate is 100% if running synced regardless of star rating, so it's almost always better to run
synced if you are able. Garuda and Shiva can be worth it to run unsynced since many groups try to do both
the achievement and spell learning at the same time which is a recipe for disaster.

 **Special Cases** 
> Spells for quests (Mind Blast, Glower) have a 100% learn rate regardless of level syncing status.
> Masked Carnivale has the unsynced learn rate for spells.

## Spell requirements for group content

**What spells are required for group content?** 
For dungeons you will want the Ram's Voice and Ultravibration to make things easier, but beyond that it mostly doesn't matter. Tanking really only requires Mighty Guard, and healing really only requires Pom Cure or Stotram. DPS benefits a _lot_ more from having more spells.

As the content gets higher level you really should try to have more spells, especially Primal spells (OGCD abilities like Feather Rain, Surpanakha, Nightbloom, etc.). Even if you just get all of your overworld spells you are doing better than most people.

 **Do I need Angel Whisper for group content?** 
For spell-learning parties it's not strictly necessary but it is helpful. For Extremes (except for spell-learning) and Savages you are griefing your party if you don't have it.

 **What role should I play in spell-learning parties?** 
Any role is fine but tank and healer don't require as many spells as DPS to be effective. There are some edge cases (e.g. O11N where tank wants Diamondback) but generally speaking in spell-learning content a weak DPS holds the party back more than the other roles.
