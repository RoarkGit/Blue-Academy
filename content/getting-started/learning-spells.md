## Learning Spells
Blue Mages learn spells by defeating enemies who cast the spell they’re trying
to learn.

### Requirements for learning spells
- The enemy casts the spell before it dies.
- The enemy actually dies.
- You are alive when the enemy dies.

### Things that affect learning rate
- Spell star rating (generally speaking, fewer stars = higher success rate)
- Level syncing (for dungeons/trials/raids, you are guaranteed to learn the
  spell when running synced)

### Things that do not affect learning rate
- Number of times the enemy casts the spell.
- Number of Blue Mages in the party.
- Having non-Blue Mages in the party.
- Party item level (minimum ilvl does nothing)
- Getting hit by the spell.
- Silencing Echo.

### Special Cases
Spells for quests (Mind Blast, Glower) have a 100% learn rate regardless of
level syncing status. Masked Carnivale has the unsynced learn rate for spells.
