Healer is a strong and viable role. With DPS dealing as much damage as it does,
you will often find yourself as the solo healer for the group, which means
healing takes clear priority over damage.

## Main Healer

The main healer drops {{< spell triple_trident >}}, {{< spell whistle >}}, and
{{< spell bristle >}} from the DPS loadout. Whistle and Triple Trident are hard
to get casts off on as a healer, and Bristle only realistically buffs
{{< spell song_of_torment >}}, which is a small enough gain that it is not
worth the slot. This leaves three open spots that can be filled by any of those
spells if you find you have the breathing room.

{{< spell-loadout loadout="CQ0UJywvOjs8P0hNTlBVWFpnaHp8AAAA" >}}

### Gameplay

Your core loop is {{< spell pom_cure >}}-ing the tank, throwing out
{{< spell gobskin >}} before incoming raid-wides, and using
{{< spell white_wind >}} as your primary heal when the party needs topping up.

{{< spell angels_snack >}} is extremely strong and should be used for big heal
checks. {{< spell stotram >}} is effective but it is the first spell to consider
dropping if you need a slot, since {{< spell pom_cure >}} into
{{< spell white_wind >}} will almost always heal for more. Because you are not
spending mana on DPS Primals the way a DPS would, you have plenty of mana to
lean on {{< spell white_wind >}} freely.

For damage, Moon Flute windows are more accessible than you might expect.
Tanks can sustain themselves with {{< spell white_wind >}} for a surprisingly
long time, so look for opportunities to squeeze in a
{{< spell moon_flute >}} window whenever the party is safe. You will mostly be
freestyling these rather than executing a tight optimized opener.

Make sure {{< spell off-guard >}} is up during every Moon Flute window to
boost the whole party's damage.

## Off-Healer

The off-healer plays essentially as a DPS that also brings
{{< spell angels_snack >}}. Angel's Snack is the primary reason to run an
off-healer at all, since it is one of the strongest healing cooldowns available.

Compared to the main healer loadout, the off-healer picks up
{{< spell tingle >}}, {{< spell whistle >}}, and {{< spell triple_trident >}},
and also adds {{< spell cold_fog >}}, which off-healers have more opportunity to
use since they are mostly playing DPS. {{< spell gobskin >}} and
{{< spell stotram >}} are dropped: the main healer handles Gobskin, and the
slot is better used elsewhere.

{{< spell-loadout loadout="CQwnLC8_QEhNTlBRUlRaZGdodnp8AABt" >}}

### Gameplay

Follow the same rotation as the DPS role. Execute Moon Flute openers with your
OGCDs whenever possible, cover big heal checks with {{< spell angels_snack >}},
and keep {{< spell off-guard >}} up for openers. Primary healing duty falls to
the main healer; if they want to Moon Flute, cover
{{< spell pom_cure >}} duty for them and alternate as the situation allows.
