{{< spell basic_instinct >}} is the key spell that makes solo dungeon runs
viable. While active, it doubles damage and healing dealt and removes the
damage penalty from {{< spell mighty_guard >}}, meaning you can be as durable
as a normal tank while hitting twice as hard.

## Mimicry

Use {{< spell aetheric_mimicry >}} on a tank. As enemies hit harder at higher
levels, the defensive boost from Tank Mimicry becomes significant. It also
augments {{< spell devour >}}, improving your survivability further.
{{< spell rehydration >}} gives you an MP-efficient way to top yourself off
without needing Healer Mimicry. That being said, Healer Mimicry is also
perfectly viable and some people prefer it. You will get hit harder, but
{{< spell pom_cure >}} becomes extremely efficient for healing yourself.

## Gameplay

Keep {{< spell basic_instinct >}} and {{< spell mighty_guard >}} active at all
times, and keep {{< spell devour >}} up when possible.
{{< spell dragon_force >}} and the Poop Scoop combo
({{< spell peat_pelt >}} into {{< spell deep_clean >}}) are strong survivability
tools worth using on larger pulls.

- **Trash:** Use {{< spell the_rams_voice >}} into {{< spell ultravibration >}}
  (the Vibe Check) whenever it is up. When it is on cooldown, use
  {{< spell breath_of_magic >}}, {{< spell apokalypsis >}},
  {{< spell phantom_flurry >}}, and other big AoE spells to clear packs.
- **Bosses:** Use {{< spell doom >}} to kill the boss instantly if it is
  vulnerable to death spells (see the
  [Death Vulnerability Table]({{% ref "death-vulnerability" %}}). Otherwise,
  use {{< spell goblin_punch >}} as your main GCD filler; with
  {{< spell mighty_guard >}} and {{< spell basic_instinct >}} active, it hits
  for an effective 640 potency per cast. Keep {{< spell breath_of_magic >}}
  rolling, use Primal spells on cooldown, and use {{< spell chelonian_gate >}}
  to mitigate tank busters. Some tank busters, especially in Shadowbringers
  content, hit extremely hard.

## Why Doom Instead of Missile?

{{< spell missile >}} is strong in group content but loses value when soloing.
Bringing a boss to kill range with Missile alone requires many casts, while
{{< spell doom >}} only needs to connect once. Doom also skips HP-triggered
phase transitions entirely, which Missile cannot do.

## Flex Spots

The last two slots in the loadout are situational. Some options:

- {{< spell white_wind >}}: Faster healing if you can manage the MP cost
- {{< spell mortal_flame >}}: Extra damage on bosses
- {{< spell moon_flute >}}: A significant damage boost on your main spells
- {{< spell sea_shanty >}}: No damage falloff on multiple targets, making it
  very strong for trash packs
- {{< spell dimensional_shift >}}: Useful for weakening enemies when Vibe Check
  is unavailable, though its slow cast time and HP-scaling damage limit how
  often you want to use it
