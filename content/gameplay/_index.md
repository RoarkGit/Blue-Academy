## Blue Mage Gameplay & Role Guides

Blue Mage is unique among FFXIV jobs because its role is determined entirely
by which spells you load into your hotbars. This flexibility allows you to
switch between tank, healer, and DPS roles without changing your character -
just adjust your loadout.

### Role-Specific Gameplay

Each role in Blue Mage has distinct mechanics and playstyles:

**Tank Role** - Build threat management and damage mitigation. Tank Blue
Mages use defensive spells and positioning to keep enemies focused on them
while maintaining survivability through healing and mitigation.

**Healer Role** - Master healing spells and support abilities. Healers
balance damage output with keeping the party alive using Blue Mage's powerful
healing toolkit.

**DPS Role** - Maximize damage output and rotational efficiency. DPS Blue
Mages focus on spell rotation optimization, burst windows, and maintaining
consistent damage throughout encounters.
