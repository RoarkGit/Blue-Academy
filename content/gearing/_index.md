## Blue Mage Gearing Guide

One of Blue Mage's unique advantages is that gear is far less important than
on other jobs. If you're struggling with content, the issue is almost
certainly not your equipment - it's typically about spell selection and
rotational optimization.

### Why Gear Matters Less on Blue Mage

Blue Mage can complete virtually all content with level-appropriate gear.
This is because:

- **Spells are the priority** - The right spell composition matters far more
  than gear quality
- **Stat variance is small** - The difference between casual and raid-tier
  gear is relatively minimal
- **Flexibility is key** - Your loadout and strategy adaptation matter more
  than stats

### When to Optimize Gear

While gear isn't mandatory, optimization becomes worthwhile for challenging
content:

- **Extreme Trials** - More challenging mechanics may benefit from optimized
  stats
- **[Savage Raids]({{% relref "/savage-guides/" %}})** - High-end raid content
  where every stat point counts
- **Personal optimization** - For players who want to squeeze every point of
  performance

Learn more about how gear interacts with different playstyles in our
[role-specific gameplay guides]({{% relref "/gameplay/" %}})
