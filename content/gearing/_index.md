Gear on Blue Mage does not actually make too much of a difference in what you're
doing. If you are struggling with something and wondering if it's related to
your gear, the answer is most likely "no." As long as you are using
level-appropriate gear you should be fine for virtually any content.

If you _do_ want to optimize more, such as for Extreme trials or Savage raids
(or just for fun), you can find more detailed info on the other gearing pages.
You can read up on what our stat priorities are and what the recommended
gearsets are.
